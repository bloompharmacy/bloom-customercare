Monthly Staff Evaluation
Bloom Customer Experience Standards — one overall score per employee, per month.
On a scale of 1–10, how consistently did this employee deliver Bloom's customer experience standards this month?
—
—
1–2 Far below · 3–4 Below · 5–6 Meets minimum · 7–8 Strong · 9 Outstanding · 10 Role model
Consider these when scoring (guidance only — not scored separately)
  • Greeting & politeness
  • Attentiveness
  • Accuracy of advice / product knowledge
  • Speed & follow-through
  • Complaint handling
  • Teamwork during peak times
  • Adherence to Bloom SOPs / appearance
One evaluation is kept per employee per month. Saving again for the same month updates that record (with an audit-log entry) — previous months are preserved.

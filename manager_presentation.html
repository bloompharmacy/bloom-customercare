Getting Started

Signing in

  1. Open the dashboard link on your computer or phone browser.
  2. Enter your username and password (provided by management).
  3. You'll land on your branch's dashboard. Your session stays active for 30 days.
💡 Tip
Use the Sign out button when using a shared computer. At the top you'll find tabs for Feedback, Staff Evaluation, and Analytics.
Feature 1

📋 Handling Customer Tickets

When a customer leaves feedback, it becomes a ticket. Your job is to review it and follow up.

  1. Open the Feedback tab — you'll see all your branch's tickets, newest first.
  2. Read the review and rating. Low ratings (1–2 ★) are flagged for quick attention.
  3. Add a note about what you did, then set the Status (New → In Progress → Resolved → Closed).
  4. Click Save. The 📋 icon shows the full activity history for that ticket.
⚠️ Complaints (1–2 ★)
These are automatically emailed to management too — so please follow up with the customer as soon as possible.
Feature 2

⚡ Recording Staff Incidents

Noticed something worth recording — good or bad? Log it the moment it happens, under Staff Evaluation → Instant Incident.

  1. Select the employee and the category (name tag, dress code, customer interaction, positive behavior…).
  2. Choose the type: Positive or Negative.
  3. Describe what happened and add any notes.
  4. Pick how to handle it, then Save.
OptionWhat happens
Record onlySaved to the employee's history. Management is not emailed.
Record & send to managementSaved and emailed to management right away.
💡 Remember
The option resets to “Record only” after each save — switch it to “send to management” whenever you want management notified.
Feature 3

⭐ Monthly Staff Evaluations

Once a month, give each team member a single score from 1 to 10 based on how consistently they delivered Bloom's customer experience standards.

How to score

Consider greeting & politeness, attentiveness, product knowledge, speed, complaint handling, teamwork, and appearance/SOPs — then give one overall score.

The scale

1–2 far below · 3–4 below · 5–6 meets minimum · 7–8 strong · 9 outstanding · 10 role model.

🔒 Important rule — the 24th deadline
You can only evaluate staff for the current month, and the window closes on the 24th. After the 24th, the form locks — you can still view, export, and submit the report, but you cannot add or change evaluations for that month. Future months are never allowed.
✅ Helpful
While scoring, the form shows that employee's incidents this month and a small score-trend chart, so you have the full picture before you decide.
Feature 4 & 5

🏆 Employee of the Month & 📊 Insights

Nominate your star

  1. In the Monthly Evaluation, tick “🏆 Nominate as Employee of the Month”, then click Save Evaluation.
  2. A “✉️ Send EOTM Nomination to HR” button appears — click it to send the nomination (with the score, your comment, and the employee's average) straight to HR.
  3. The form also shows last month's winner for your branch, so you can keep things fair.
📊 The Analytics tab
Open the Analytics tab to see clean charts: your team's average score by employee and the monthly trend. It's a great way to spot who's improving and who might need support.
Feature 6

📤 Monthly Reports & Exports

At month's end, send your branch's summary to management — the system builds the Excel file and emails it for you.

  1. Go to Staff Evaluation → History.
  2. Pick the report month and click Submit Monthly Report.
  3. Confirm — management receives the full Excel report automatically.

💡 Already sent?
If you submit the same month twice, the system asks you to confirm before resending — so you never send duplicates by accident.
Quick Reference

Handy tips & common questions

QuestionAnswer
I can't add an evaluation — why?It's likely after the 24th, or you selected a past/future month. Only the current month is editable, through the 24th.
How do I nominate Employee of the Month?Tick the 🏆 box, Save, then click “Send EOTM Nomination to HR”.
Where do I see performance trends?The Analytics tab — plus a mini trend chart under each evaluation.
Do complaints reach management?Yes — 1–2★ tickets are emailed automatically. You still follow up with the customer.
Can I see other branches?No — you only see your own branch. That's by design.
Where do reports go?Straight to management's inbox as an Excel file. You can also export your own copy.
✅ Golden rule
Log incidents when they happen, evaluate (and nominate) before the 24th, and submit your report at month's end.
